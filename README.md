# SQL Sales Analysis
B2B Sales Analytics · Revenue & Operations

## Overview
Analyzed a $23.1M B2B sales database to identify revenue drivers, operational inefficiencies, and assess acquisition viability.

## Methods
- SQL joins across 5 relational tables
- Aggregations and segmentation analysis
- Revenue and regional performance analysis

## Key Findings
- Revenue reached $23.1M with 216% growth
- Sales rep allocation was uneven, with high-opportunity regions underserved
- Finance & Insurance and Energy were the strongest customer segments
- Operational inefficiencies reduced overall performance potential
