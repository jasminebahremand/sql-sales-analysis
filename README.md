# SQL Sales Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed a $23.1M B2B sales database to evaluate growth, product mix, and regional performance. Identified inefficiencies in sales rep allocation and uncovered key revenue drivers across products and regions.

## Key Findings
- Revenue reached $23.1M with 216% growth
- Standard paper was the top product by revenue and volume
- Regional rep allocation was uneven, leaving high-opportunity regions underserved
- Finance & Insurance and Energy were the strongest customer segments
